## Premise under pressure

**Claim:** People in temporary hardship will use a ZK voting circle to prioritize bills privately with trusted supporters.

## Hard questions

1. **Why now?** Midnight makes private verified coordination possible; hardship + public crowdfunding stigma is not new — the enabler is the proof primitive.
2. **Why you?** Team shipped real Compact circuits + Lace Preprod path; honesty about demo limits is a trust advantage.
3. **Who screams for this?** Not “everyone in debt”. Narrow: people with a ready trust circle who refuse public campaigns.
4. **What if WhatsApp is enough?** Then Lifeline only wins when fairness disputes appear — size the segment where disputes hurt.
5. **Are you building for judges or users?** MVP is judge-legible; LoFA A1–A3 decide if users care.
